University of South Carolina Football Gameday Essentials

Everything you need for a Saturday in Williams-Brice Stadium and Cocky's Cockaboose!

The Gamecock Gameday Checklist

  • Garnet and Black Apparel: Jersey, t-shirt, or polo in school colors.
  • Gamecock Spirit Gear: Shaker (pom-pom) for waving during the Fight Song.
  • Tailgate Supplies: Tent, folding chairs, cooler, and food for Cockaboose Railroad tailgating.
  • The "2001: A Space Odyssey" Track: Be ready to cheer when the lights flash and the team runs out to the iconic intro.
  • Sandstorm Towel: Essential for twirling during the legendary crowd hype song.
  • Clear Bag: Complying with Williams-Brice Stadium bag policies.
  • Mobile Ticket: Loaded on your smartphone for stadium entry.
  • Sunscreen & Sunglasses: For those hot early-season afternoon kickoffs.
  • Rain Poncho: Just in case an afternoon shower hits Columbia.
  • The Fight Song Knowledge: Ready to sing "Go Gamecocks Go!" after every score.
